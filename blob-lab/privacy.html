Privacy Policy – Blob Lab

Last updated: September 2026

Blob Lab ("the app") is developed by Jovate Games. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email or contacts. Your game progress (blobs, coins, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (gems, gold, a welcome pack and ad removal). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. We only receive an anonymous confirmation of the purchase so the item can be delivered. Everything that can be bought can also be earned by playing for free.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app usage information and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy" button in the app's main menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
